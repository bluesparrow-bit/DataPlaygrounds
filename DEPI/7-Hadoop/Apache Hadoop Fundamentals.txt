2- Very high fault tolerance
Mechanism =>> What it does
Replication =>> Every block is stored 3 times on different nodes
Heartbeats =>> Each DataNode tells the NameNode "I'm alive" every 3 seconds
Dead node detection =>> No heartbeat for about 10 minutes means the node is marked dead
Re-replication =>> The NameNode makes new copies of the lost blocks on healthy nodes, so there are 3 copies again
Checksums =>> Detect corrupted blocks. A bad copy is replaced from a good one.
NameNode High Availability =>> A Standby NameNode takes over if the active one fails (coordinated by ZooKeeper)

3-Rack awareness
What's a rack? A cabinet holding about 20–40 servers that share one network switch and often one power supply. If the switch or power fails, the whole rack goes down.
Rack awareness =>> means the NameNode knows which rack each DataNode is in and uses that when placing copies.
